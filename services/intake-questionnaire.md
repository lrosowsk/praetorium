# Intake questionnaire: AI in OT Risk Assessment

Please return within 5 business days. Short answers are fine; “don’t know” is a useful answer too. We’ll go deeper in interviews.

**Organization:** ______  **Completed by (name, role):** ______  **Date:** ______

## A. Your environment
1. Briefly describe your operations (e.g. generation, transmission, distribution, water, manufacturing) and number of sites.
2. Which control system software do you run, and from which vendors? (SCADA, EMS/DMS/ADMS, historian, asset management, PLC programming tools, building management)
3. How is your OT network separated from your corporate IT network? Is there an OT demilitarized zone (DMZ)?
4. How do vendors and remote staff access OT systems today?
5. Do you run OT security monitoring (e.g. Dragos, Claroty, Nozomi)? If so, which, and where?
6. Are you subject to NERC CIP? If so, at what impact levels?

## B. AI in your organization
7. Do you have a policy covering AI use? Does it say anything specific about operational systems?
8. Which AI tools are approved for staff (e.g. ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Claude, Gemini, coding assistants)?
9. Do engineers use AI coding assistants or agents for scripts, configurations, PLC logic or analysis? On which machines?
10. Have any of your control system vendors added AI features (assistants, “copilots”, optimization, anomaly detection)? Are they turned on?
11. Do you run in-house or third-party models that read operational data (forecasting, predictive maintenance, outage prediction)? Do their outputs drive any action automatically?
12. Can any AI system today **write** to, or send commands to, an operational system, directly or through another tool? If unsure, say so.
13. Are any AI tools connected to systems through APIs, plugins, automation platforms or MCP servers?

## C. Governance and oversight
14. Who approves new software or features in OT? Does that process cover AI features that arrive through vendor updates?
15. If an AI tool did something unexpected in OT, how would you find out, and who could stop it?
16. Have auditors, regulators, your board or your insurer asked about AI? What did they ask?
17. Have you had any incidents or near misses involving automation or AI?

## D. Goals
18. What would make this assessment a success for you?
19. Are there specific AI tools, vendors or projects you’re most concerned about?
20. Any upcoming deadlines (audits, board meetings, insurance renewals, AI rollouts) we should work towards?
