# AI in OT Risk Assessment

**A fixed-price assessment that finds every AI system able to reach a utility’s or manufacturer’s control systems, works out what each one could physically do, and delivers the policy, roadmap and evidence auditors and boards are starting to ask for.**

It’s the first revenue line for Preceptor Defense. It builds credibility, gives us real data on how AI shows up in operational technology (OT), and leads naturally to the Preceptor product.

## The problem it solves for the customer

- AI is arriving in OT through vendor software updates, engineers’ AI tools and analytics projects, often without security review.
- Nobody can answer “Which AI tools can reach our control systems, and what could they do?”
- Boards, insurers and auditors are starting to ask. NERC CIP-015 requires monitoring inside control networks by 2028, and EU and US state AI rules are arriving.
- Existing OT security tools watch traffic and devices. They don’t inventory AI or assess what it’s allowed to do.

## What the customer gets

| Deliverable | What it is |
|---|---|
| **AI inventory** | Every AI system that touches or can reach OT: vendor AI features, engineers’ AI tools, analytics models, and unapproved “shadow AI”. Who owns it, what it can read and write, how it connects. |
| **Consequence-based risk register** | Each AI path rated by what it could physically cause (from “reads data” to “could change setpoints or open breakers”) and how likely misuse is. |
| **Lab-tested findings** | The highest-risk AI paths reproduced in our simulated substation, never on the customer’s live systems, showing concretely what could go wrong. |
| **AI-in-OT policy** | A ready-to-adopt policy: what AI may read, what needs human approval, what AI may never touch, and how vendors must disclose AI features. |
| **30/90/365-day roadmap** | Quick wins first, then controls to put in place, prioritized by risk reduction. |
| **Compliance mapping** | How findings and controls relate to NERC CIP (where applicable), the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, and the EU AI Act. |
| **Executive readout** | A board-ready summary and a 60-minute briefing for leadership. |

## How we work (and why it’s safe)

- **Passive and read-only.** We review documents, configurations, logs and existing sensor data. We never install anything in, or send traffic into, live control networks.
- **Testing happens in our lab.** Risky scenarios are reproduced in our simulated substation, not on the customer’s equipment.
- **Data stays with the customer.** We work on customer-provided exports or on-site, under an NDA and agreed rules of engagement (`rules-of-engagement.md`).

## Who delivers it

- A lead assessor with OT/ICS security experience (the grid-security co-founder, or a contracted OT consultant until then).
- The founder, as AI and agent security lead and engagement manager.
- Optional: a compliance specialist for NERC CIP-heavy customers.

## How it leads to the product

Every assessment ends with a roadmap. Its top recommendations, an AI inventory kept current, per-agent rules, human approval for risky actions, and audit evidence, are what Preceptor automates. Offer the Preceptor watch-only pilot as the natural next step in the readout.
