# Rules of engagement: AI in OT Risk Assessment

**Customer:** [Organization]  **Engagement:** [Snapshot / Full Assessment]  **Dates:** [start] to [end]

These rules govern how Preceptor Defense (“we”) performs the assessment. Both parties sign before work starts. *Template only: have counsel review before use.*

## 1. Scope
- **In scope:** [sites, systems, business units].
- **Out of scope:** [systems excluded, e.g. specific plants, nuclear assets, safety instrumented systems].

## 2. What we will do
- Review documents, diagrams, configurations and policies the customer provides.
- Analyze log and monitoring exports the customer provides (DNS, proxy, firewall, OT monitoring data).
- Interview the customer’s staff and, with the customer’s permission, its vendors.
- Reproduce selected scenarios **in our own lab environment**, using simulated equipment.
- Facilitate a tabletop exercise (Full Assessment only).

## 3. What we will not do
- Connect any device to, install any software on, or send any traffic into the customer’s operational networks.
- Run scans, exploits or tests against the customer’s live systems.
- Access customer systems with our own credentials. All data is provided by the customer as exports or viewed on-site under supervision.
- Share customer data with any third party, or put it into third-party AI tools, without written permission.

## 4. Data handling
- Customer data is stored encrypted, in a dedicated engagement folder, accessible only to named assessors: [names].
- Data is [returned / securely deleted] within [30] days of the final report, with written confirmation.
- Anonymized, non-attributable findings may be used to improve our methods [only with written consent / as agreed in the design-partner terms].

## 5. Communication and escalation
- Customer sponsor: [name, title, phone].
- Day-to-day contact: [name, title, phone].
- **Urgent findings** (e.g. an AI system able to operate safety equipment or breakers without human approval, or signs of compromise) are reported to the sponsor **by phone the same day**.
