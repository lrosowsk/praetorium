# Preceptor Defense: strategy on one page

## What we are
A checkpoint between AI and the physical world. Every AI request to a utility’s control systems passes through Preceptor: safe actions go through, risky ones wait for a human, dangerous ones are blocked, and everything is recorded as evidence for regulators.

## Who we sell to first
Smaller US power companies: generation-and-transmission co-ops, joint action agencies and city-owned utilities. They fall under federal grid-security rules, decide faster than large utilities, and each one reaches dozens of others. Target list: `discovery/prospects.csv`.

## How we win
1. **Build on incumbents, don’t fight them.** Dragos, Claroty and Nozomi own network monitoring. Their 2026 AI products (EmberAI, Claire, Vantage IQ) are assistants for defenders. None identifies which AI agent acted, sets rules per agent, or holds an AI action for human approval. We use their sensors and add that layer.
2. **Neutrality.** Utilities running AI from several vendors want one independent checkpoint, not each vendor grading its own AI.
3. **Let AI act safely, don’t just stop it.** IT-focused “AI kill switches” (CrowdStrike, Straiker, JetStream) only shut agents down. We approve, block or hold, based on what the action does physically.
4. **Low-risk deployment.** Step 1: gate the AI tools the utility controls (software only). Step 2: watch-only, using existing sensors. Step 3: enforce once trusted, failing safe. Everything on-premises.
5. **Compliance pulls the budget.** US regulators require monitoring inside control networks by 2028. AI evidence rides on that obligation.

## Biggest risks, and what we do about them
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Dragos or Claroty adds AI-agent governance | Move fast, integrate with them, own the approval workflow, and treat them as likely acquirers |
| AI isn’t deep in utility control systems yet | Lead with compliance evidence and AI-tool governance, which utilities need now |
| AI built into vendor grid software looks like the software itself | Behavioral correlation now; vendor partnerships and certification later |
| Long utility approval and sales cycles | Start with faster small utilities, sell through co-ops and agencies, use grants and pilot fees |
| Credibility with utilities | Grid-security co-founder, national lab advisors, watch-only first |
| Name not yet cleared | USPTO search, domain check and attorney opinion before launch (`research/name-check.md`) |
