## AI and the control room: a research project

**[Your name] · [email] · [phone]**

### What we’re exploring

AI is reaching utility operations from three directions at once:

- **Vendor AI.** Copilots and AI features are arriving inside SCADA, EMS, ADMS and asset-management software, often through routine updates.
- **Engineers’ AI tools.** Coding assistants and AI agents are being connected to historians, configuration systems and engineering workstations.
- **AI-driven attacks.** In 2025, the first intrusion campaign run mostly by an AI agent was made public, and US agencies have warned that state actors are already inside utility networks.

At the same time, regulators are raising the bar. FERC’s 2025 approval of NERC CIP-015 requires monitoring inside control networks, and EU rules for high-risk AI in critical infrastructure are due to apply in late 2027.

### The questions we’re trying to answer

1. Where is AI actually showing up in and around operational systems today?
2. Can security teams see and control what AI tools can reach?
3. How are compliance programs, auditors, boards and insurers starting to treat AI?
4. What would good look like, and what would it be worth?
