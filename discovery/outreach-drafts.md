# Outreach drafts: first 15 prospects

Ready-to-send drafts for the first 15 organizations in `prospects.md`. Before sending each one:

1. **Find the person.** Use the organization’s leadership page or LinkedIn and look for the roles listed in `prospects.csv` (CISO, OT security manager, NERC CIP compliance manager; at smaller utilities, the IT manager). Replace `[First name]`.
2. **Add one real detail** where you see `[personal line]`: something they said in a talk, post or article, a mutual connection, or a recent project. Leave it out rather than invent one.
3. **Check it reads as research, not sales.** The goal is a 25-minute conversation. Don’t attach the deck. Offer the one-page brief (`06-one-page-brief.md`, exported to PDF) only if they ask.
4. **Log it** in `target-list.csv` the same day. Follow up after 4–5 business days and again a week later (templates at the bottom).
