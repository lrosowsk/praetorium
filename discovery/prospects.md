# Prospect list

`prospects.csv` lists 61 organizations to contact first, sorted by priority. It names **organizations and the roles to ask for**, not individual people. Find the right person on each organization's leadership page or LinkedIn, then fill in `contact_name` and `contact_title`. Move rows into `target-list.csv` as you start talking to them.

The list was compiled from general industry knowledge in November 2026. Check each organization's website before reaching out, since names, mergers and structures change.

## How it's prioritized

**Priority 1: G&T cooperatives and joint action agencies (24).** These are the best first targets, for two reasons:
- **NERC CIP applies to them.** G&Ts and large public power own the generation and transmission assets the CIP standards cover, so the compliance-evidence pitch hits a real, funded obligation. Most distribution co-ops have little CIP burden.
- **One relationship reaches many utilities.** A G&T serves dozens of member co-ops and a joint action agency serves dozens to 100+ municipal utilities. Either can become a channel.

**Priority 2: large public power and larger distribution co-ops (28).** These have bigger security teams and, in several cases, a reputation for adopting new technology early (marked "Likely early adopter" or "Possible design partner" in the notes). Utilities that also run water systems are marked "Power + water", since they fit the expansion plan.

**Priority 3: associations, labs and funders (9).** Not buyers, but they provide credibility, introductions, grants and threat insight. Contact them early: one good relationship at NRECA, APPA or a national lab can open many doors.

## Suggested first 15

Mix the segments so your first interviews cover the range:

1. Three or four G&Ts (for example Basin Electric, Great River Energy, Old Dominion, Oglethorpe Power).
2. Three joint action agencies (for example American Municipal Power, Florida Municipal Power Agency, MEAG Power).
3. Three early-adopter utilities (EPB Chattanooga, Holy Cross Energy, Roanoke Electric Cooperative).
4. Two large public power utilities (for example SMUD and Salt River Project).
5. One each from NRECA, APPA and Idaho National Laboratory.

## Outreach

Use the templates in `03-outreach-templates.md`. A warm introduction beats cold email with utilities, so ask each association contact and every interviewee who else you should talk to.
