Source: Cisco, 2026 State of Industrial AI: Utilities (vendor survey)

[60s] Start with the problem. Sixty-two percent of utilities say they’re already rolling out AI at scale, and that security is what’s holding them back. AI is showing up in three ways. It’s built into the software that runs the grid, often switched on by a routine update. Engineers are connecting AI tools to systems that sit right next to the controls. And if you ask a utility, “What did your AI touch last week?”, nobody can answer. There’s no list, no rules and no record a regulator would accept. Here’s the key point: in most industries an AI mistake leaks data. Here, it causes a blackout.
