01 · The problem

AI is moving into the control room faster than anyone can govern it.

62%

of utilities say they are already deploying AI at scale, and that security gaps are what limit it.

Vendor AI inside OT software

Copilots are shipping inside SCADA, EMS and asset-management tools, often switched on by an update.

Agents with real credentials

Engineers connect coding agents and AI tools to historians, ticketing and config systems that sit next to the controls.

No answer to “what did the AI touch?”

Security teams have no inventory of AI, no policy for it, and no audit trail a regulator would accept.

Source: Cisco, 2026 State of Industrial AI: Utilities (vendor survey)

The point: AI adoption in utilities isn’t hypothetical any more. Even a vendor survey we should treat with caution says most utilities are deploying it, and that security is the brake. The three cards are the three paths AI takes toward the controls. Customer interviews will tell us which one hurts most; update this slide with real quotes once we have them.
