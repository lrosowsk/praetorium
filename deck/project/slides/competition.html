Dragos, Claroty and Nozomi all launched AI products this year, but they’re assistants that help defenders. None of them governs the AI that’s acting on the grid, and none can hold an AI action for human approval. So we build on top of them: we use their sensors, and add what they don’t do. The honest risk is that a big vendor bolts on an AI feature. Our answer is to be the neutral checkpoint across every vendor, and to move fast.
