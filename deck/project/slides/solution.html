What we do

Preceptor is a checkpoint: AI can’t change anything physical without passing through us.

01

Find

List every AI system that can reach the grid, including ones nobody approved.

02

Set rules

“This AI can read meter data, needs a human to approve voltage changes, and can never touch a breaker.”

03

Catch

Spot AI behaving like an attacker, such as scanning the network at machine speed, and lock it out.

04

Prove

Keep a tamper-proof record of what AI did and who approved it, for regulators and the board.

Preceptor Defense · Confidential

Land with Discover and Prove: visibility and compliance evidence are what get budget today, and they run in observe-only mode, so there is no risk to operations. Enforce and Detect are the expansion as AI moves deeper into operations.
