04 · The product

Tagout is a policy layer between AI and the systems that move power.

01

Discover

Find every AI agent, model API call and tool connection that reaches operational systems, including vendor AI nobody approved.

02

Enforce

Apply rules in operators’ terms: this agent may read the historian, never write a setpoint, and needs a human to approve any switching.

03

Detect

Spot machine-speed reconnaissance and command patterns that look like an AI agent, not an operator, and contain them.

04

Prove

Produce the audit trail boards and regulators ask for: what AI touched what, under which policy, and who approved it.

Tagout · Confidential

Land with Discover and Prove: visibility and compliance evidence are what get budget today, and they run in observe-only mode, so there is no risk to operations. Enforce and Detect are the expansion as AI moves deeper into operations.
