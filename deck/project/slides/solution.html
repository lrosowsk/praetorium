Preceptor Defense · Confidential

[45s] Concretely, Preceptor does four things. It finds every AI system that can reach the grid, including ones nobody approved. It lets the utility set rules in plain language: this AI can read meter data, needs a human to approve voltage changes, and can never touch a breaker. It catches AI behaving like an attacker and locks it out. And it keeps a tamper-proof record, so the utility can prove to regulators and its board that AI is under control.
