Land

Governance and compliance at co-ops and municipal utilities, via associations and the providers that already serve them.

Expand

More modules per customer, then new sectors. Services fund the early years and build trust.

Preceptor Defense · Confidential

Utilities are the core revenue. The other four customer types are what make this bigger than a utility software company: vendors pay to prove their AI is safe, insurers pay for risk data, government funds research and protects its own sites, and security providers resell to the thousands of small utilities we could never reach one by one. Pricing for each still needs to come out of discovery.
