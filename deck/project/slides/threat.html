02 · The threat

Attackers already let AI agents do most of the work.

80–90%

of the hands-on intrusion work in a 2025 state-sponsored espionage campaign was carried out by an AI agent.

~30

organizations targeted in that one campaign, including chemical manufacturers and government agencies.

Inside

US agencies warned in 2024 that Volt Typhoon, a Chinese state group, was hiding inside US energy, water and transport networks.

Sources: Anthropic, GTG-1002 report (Nov 2025); CISA advisory on Volt Typhoon (2024)

Machine-speed attacks change the math for defenders. A human attacker probes a few systems a day; an agent can probe hundreds and adapt as it goes. Combine that with adversaries who are already inside utility networks and the case for controls built for AI-speed activity makes itself.
