[45s] And it isn’t just mistakes. Last year Anthropic disclosed a state-sponsored hacking campaign where an AI agent did 80 to 90 percent of the work, against about 30 organizations. US agencies have also warned that Chinese state hackers are already hiding inside American power and water networks. Put those together and you get attackers who are already inside, now with AI that moves faster than any human defender.
