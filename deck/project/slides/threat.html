The threat

Hackers use AI too, and it attacks at machine speed.

80–90%

of the hacking in a 2025 state-sponsored attack campaign was done by an AI agent, not people.

~30

organizations targeted in that one campaign, including chemical makers and government agencies.

Inside

US agencies warned in 2024 that Chinese state hackers were already hiding in US power, water and transport networks.

Sources: Anthropic, GTG-1002 report (Nov 2025); CISA advisory on Volt Typhoon (2024)

Machine-speed attacks change the math for defenders. A human attacker probes a few systems a day; an agent can probe hundreds and adapt as it goes. Combine that with adversaries who are already inside utility networks and the case for controls built for AI-speed activity makes itself.
