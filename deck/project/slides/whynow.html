Why now

New rules and new threats are arriving at the same time.

2025

US regulators order power companies to monitor activity inside their control networks. Deadline: 2028.

2025

The first known cyberattack campaign run mostly by AI is made public.

2026

Utilities move AI from experiments into daily operations.

2027

EU rules for high-risk AI, including AI in critical infrastructure, are due to apply.

Utilities must start watching inside their networks, and AI is now one of the things in there.

Sources: FERC Order 907 (NERC CIP-015); Anthropic; Cisco; EU Digital Omnibus provisional agreement (May 2026)

[40s] Why now? Two forces are arriving together. On the rules side, US regulators have ordered power companies to monitor activity inside their control networks, with a 2028 deadline, and Europe’s rules for high-risk AI are coming. On the threat side, the first AI-run cyberattacks are public, and utilities are moving AI from experiments into daily operations. Utilities have to start watching inside their networks, and AI is now one of the things in there.
