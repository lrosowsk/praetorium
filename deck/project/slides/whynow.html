08 · Why now

The rules and the risk are arriving at the same time.

Jun 2025

FERC approves NERC CIP-015: utilities must monitor traffic inside their control networks, with compliance due by October 2028.

Nov 2025

The first documented intrusion campaign run mostly by an AI agent is made public.

2026

Utilities move AI from pilots into production, and vendors embed agents in operational software.

Dec 2027

EU AI Act rules for high-risk AI, including critical infrastructure, are due to apply.

Monitoring inside the control network is becoming mandatory. AI is becoming one of the things on it.

Sources: FERC Order 907; Anthropic; Cisco; EU Digital Omnibus provisional agreement (May 2026)

Two forces converge: regulators now require utilities to watch inside their control networks, and AI is becoming one of the things on those networks. The EU date reflects the Digital Omnibus deal that pushed the high-risk deadline back; confirm the final adopted date before presenting.
