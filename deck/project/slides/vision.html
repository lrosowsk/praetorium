04 · The vision

Preceptor makes it safe to let software act on the physical world.

Today

AI agents and copilots

Reaching into grid control systems through vendor software and engineers’ tools.

Next

Automation and vendor access

Scripts, integrations and third-party technicians, governed by the same hold points.

Tomorrow

Autonomous machines

Robots, drones and self-running plants across water, pipelines and transport.

Preceptor Defense · Confidential

This is the slide that turns a feature into a company. More and more software is getting permission to move physical things: open valves, switch breakers, dispatch crews, fly drones. Every one of those actors needs a safety and control layer. AI on the grid is where the need is sharpest today, so that’s where we start.
