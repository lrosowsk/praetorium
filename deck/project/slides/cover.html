The name: a praetorium was the Roman commander’s headquarters, where orders were authorized before anyone acted on them. That’s what we are for AI. The product uses a term every utility engineer knows: a hold point is the step in a work procedure that nobody may pass until a qualified person signs off. We put hold points in front of AI. Agents can read and recommend, but anything that could change the physical grid stops and waits for a human.
