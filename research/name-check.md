# Name check: Praetorium Defense

Researched November 2026 from public trademark aggregators (Justia, uspto.report, Markinton, TrademarkDir) and web search. **This is not legal advice and not a full clearance search.** Confirm everything in the USPTO's own Trademark Search and get a trademark attorney's opinion before filing or spending on branding.

## Bottom line

**High risk.** No one appears to have registered "PRAETORIUM", but **PRAETORIAN** is a live, registered mark for cybersecurity services, owned by an established security company. The two words look and sound alike, and the services overlap directly. Under US trademark law (likelihood of confusion, Lanham Act §2(d)), that combination is the classic reason an application gets refused, or a company gets a cease-and-desist letter.

## Domains

This session's network blocks domain registration lookups (RDAP/WHOIS), so domains are **unchecked**. Check `praetorium.com`, `praetoriumdefense.com`, `praetorium.ai` and `praetorium.io` at any registrar. Web search found no live site at praetorium.com: every result redirected to praetorian.com, which shows how easily the two get confused.

## Options

1. **Keep Praetorium and get a lawyer's opinion first.** An attorney may argue "Defense" plus the critical-infrastructure focus distinguishes it, but expect the risk to be called meaningful. A clearance opinion typically costs a few hundred to a couple of thousand dollars.
2. **Switch to a cleaner Latin name.** In earlier checks, **Custos** ("guard") had no security, defense or AI company using it. The only US CUSTOS registration found is in Class 36 (financial services), plus a 2026 UK application covering healthcare AI software. **Vindex** and **Versor** also came back clean on company searches. Each still needs a proper trademark search.
3. **Use Praetorium as an internal codename** while you validate the market, and pick the public name before incorporating or launching a website.
